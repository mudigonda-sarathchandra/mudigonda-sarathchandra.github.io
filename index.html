About Me

Research interests: Climate Risk, Trade and Industrial Development

I am supervised by Professor Hélène Ollivier and am currently an Eiffel Excellence Doctoral Scholar.

I hold a BSc in Economics from Shiv Nadar University (with a semester at the University of Bologna) and an MRes in Economics from PSE. Prior to my doctoral studies, I worked at the Structural Policy and Research Division at the OECD Economics Department (2023–2024).

Outside of research, I enjoy bouldering and playing ultimate frisbee. If you're ever in Paris, feel free to join our pickup frisbee games through the Good Vibes Paris group, or drop me a line if you'd like to catch a Manchester United match together.
