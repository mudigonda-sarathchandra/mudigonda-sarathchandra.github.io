I am a PhD candidate in Economics at the Paris School of Economics (PSE), supervised by Professor Hélène Ollivier. My research focuses on climate risk and industrial development in developing economies, with a sustained empirical focus on India's Special Economic Zone (SEZ) system. You can find my CV here.

Research

Working Papers

  • [Job Market Paper] SEZ Placement and Flood Risk in India — A quantitative spatial general equilibrium study of India's SEZ policy and flood risk, combining a staggered difference-in-differences design with an Eaton-Kortum trade model. PDF
  • The Golden Quadrilateral Highway and Intra-Sector Misallocation (with François Fontaine and Samiran Dutta) — Uses an incidental-placement IV design to study the effects of GQ highway exposure on firm outcomes. PDF
  • Production Networks and Flood Shocks (with Hélène Ollivier, Geoffrey Barrows, and Tushar K. Nandi) — PDF

Work in Progress
